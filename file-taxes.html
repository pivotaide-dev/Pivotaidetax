Checklist

What to gather before you start

Having these ready ensures your return is filed without delays.

Identity

Identification

  • Driver’s license or state ID for all filers
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters
  • Identity Protection PIN (IP PIN) if issued
  • Direct deposit routing and account numbers
Income

Income Documents

  • W-2 forms from all employers
  • 1099-NEC / 1099-K / 1099-MISC
  • 1099-INT / 1099-DIV / 1099-B brokerage forms
  • K-1s from partnerships, S-Corps, or trusts
Deductions

Deductions & Credits

  • Form 1098 Mortgage Interest statement
  • Real estate and personal property tax bills
  • 1098-T Tuition or 1098-E student loan interest
  • Prior year federal and state tax returns (copy)
